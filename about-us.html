Our Story

KIVO is a modern, Philippine-developed point-of-sale and inventory management system designed for MSMEs seeking a smarter and more efficient way to manage daily operations. It provides a seamless experience by integrating sales processing, real-time inventory tracking, financial insights, employee management, and customer engagement into one intuitive platform.


Built with BIR-compliant documentation, secure cloud backup, and omnichannel capabilities, KIVO supports both small-medium enterprises, and multi-branch businesses. It is simple enough for new entrepreneurs yet robust enough for growing enterprises, ensuring that every transaction, report, and decision becomes clearer, faster, and more reliable.

What is Kivo?

Founded in 2025, KIVO was created to address a common challenge faced by Filipino MSMEs—manual processes that slow operations, cause errors, and limit business growth. Our team recognized the need for an intelligent yet accessible system that could streamline sales, inventory, and financial management without overwhelming users.


KIVO was developed with a clear purpose: to empower entrepreneurs through smart, efficient, and data-driven tools. Today, it stands as a modern Filipino-built POS solution designed to simplify operations, support informed decision-making, and help businesses thrive in an increasingly digital economy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Kivo work?

Kivo automates sales, inventory updates, and financial reporting. Once you record a sale, all related data is updated instantly across your connected modules.

Is Kivo secure?

Absolutely. Kivo uses encrypted cloud technology to ensure that all your business data is stored safely and accessible only by authorized users.

Do I need an internet connection to use Kivo?

Kivo can work both online and offline. Your data automatically syncs to the cloud once a stable connection is detected.

Can I access Kivo on mobile?

Yes! Kivo is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices, ensuring you stay in control wherever you are.
