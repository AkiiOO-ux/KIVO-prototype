Our Story

Kivo was founded in 2021 with a vision to simplify the way small and medium businesses manage their sales, inventory, and finances. What started as a basic POS concept quickly evolved into an all-in-one smart platform that empowers entrepreneurs to run their operations efficiently — anywhere, anytime.

What is Kivo?

Kivo is a cloud-based Point of Sale and Business Management System designed to help stores streamline transactions, track real-time inventory, and generate financial insights. With its simple interface and powerful analytics, Kivo is your trusted partner in building a smarter, more connected business.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Kivo work?

Kivo automates sales, inventory updates, and financial reporting. Once you record a sale, all related data is updated instantly across your connected modules.

Is Kivo secure?

Absolutely. Kivo uses encrypted cloud technology to ensure that all your business data is stored safely and accessible only by authorized users.

Do I need an internet connection to use Kivo?

Kivo can work both online and offline. Your data automatically syncs to the cloud once a stable connection is detected.

Can I access Kivo on mobile?

Yes! Kivo is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices, ensuring you stay in control wherever you are.
